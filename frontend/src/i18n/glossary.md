# English glossary — Prakasa Workspace

Tone: concise product English, **sentence case** ("Payment requests", not "Payment Requests"), like the Google Admin console.
No exclamation marks, no "please". Keep punctuation and trailing colons/periods as in the Indonesian. British/Indonesian
number formats are not your concern (the app formats numbers and dates itself).
